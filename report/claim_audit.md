# Claim audit for Technical Research Report v1

| Claim | Supporting experiment/data | Evidence strength | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Linear Search found large improvements early and then entered a flatter late regime. | Full 40-iteration TSV: 1.904472 baseline, 1.324037 first-30 best, 1.320959 full-run best. | Direct observation | One adaptive trajectory; “plateau” describes this curve rather than a universal stopping point. |
| The standardized Linear view shows 30 iterations while the original run contained 40. | `full_40_iterations.tsv`, `standardized_first_30.tsv`, metadata annotation. | Direct observation | The final ten iterations remain relevant for the full-run best. |
| Uniform branching alone did not recover the recorded root baseline in the Tree run. | Uniform Random Tree: best 2.002502 versus root 1.885642; 0/30 below root. | Direct observation | Does not establish that tree search is generally ineffective. |
| Uniform Random Tree contained local recoveries. | 9/30 children had lower BPB than their parent. | Direct observation | Local recovery did not translate into a root-baseline win in this run. |
| Uniform Random DAG produced a real two-parent topology. | 15 valid Expands, 15 valid Merges; audited source edges. | Direct observation | Structural validity does not imply optimization benefit. |
| No completed Merge in Uniform Random DAG beat its better parent. | 0/15 parent-relative Merge improvements. | Direct observation | Only one trajectory and 15 Merges. |
| Credit-Guided Parent DAG replaced uniform allocation with quality, child-improvement, and exploration credit. | Stored `sample.py`, `credit_guidance`, and experiment report. | Direct observation | This verifies the mechanism, not its superiority. |
| Credit can reward a node for useful direct descendants even when the node is not currently best. | Implemented positive direct-child improvement term `E(v)` in credit calculation. | Direct observation | Only direct children contribute; delayed multi-generation value is not modeled. |
| Credit-Guided Parent DAG did not hard-prune inactive or low-quality nodes. | Eligibility condition includes every finished node with finite result; `program.md`. | Direct observation | Stochastic softmax can still assign very small probability to poor nodes. |
| Credit-guided allocation did not by itself solve proposal concentration. | 29/30 finished proposals in the experiment audit concerned the `MATRIX_LR` lineage. | Direct observation | The cause could include prompt, agent, starting state, or search-space effects. |
| Merge pair incompatibility caused execution-level starvation in the first credit-guided run. | 31 semantic rejects among 36 Merge attempts; four completed Merges. | Direct observation | “Incompatibility” follows the stored semantic rejection process. |
| Bounded pair resampling reduced Merge starvation. | Completed Merge conversion changed from 4/36 to 12/19 in the adjacent mechanism experiments. | Direct observation | Separate adaptive runs; comparison describes realized execution, not a general causal effect size. |
| More completed Merges did not guarantee optimization improvement. | Merge-Retry run: 12 Merges, only 1 beat its better parent; 0/30 beat root baseline. | Direct observation | Does not rule out benefit at larger budgets or with better synthesis. |
| Outcome-Aware Proposal-Memory DAG recorded broader mechanism coverage and more local improvements than the preceding trajectory. | 18 mechanism families; 10/30 local improvements, including 4/10 Merges. | Direct observation | Cross-run difference is not a causal estimate; multiple proposal mechanisms changed together. |
| Proposal memory may help address proposal collapse. | Structured memory/diversity intervention and observed 18-family trajectory. | Suggestive | Single run, bundled intervention, and no frozen-context control. |
| Outcome-Blind Proposal-Memory DAG was not memoryless. | It retained family attempts, transitions, novelty information, retry context, code/diff context, and credit-guided selection. | Direct observation | It removed explicit proposal-outcome labels, not all history or all outcome use. |
| Outcome-Blind Proposal-Memory DAG retained broad proposal coverage. | 21 mechanism families across 30 valid candidates. | Direct observation | Family taxonomy is an analysis convention and cannot establish causality. |
| Most Outcome-Blind root-baseline wins were inherited rather than new local gains. | 28/30 below root; 9 local improvements; 19 classified as inherited success. | Direct observation | Classification depends on the stored parent topology and local-success convention. |
| Credit-guided selection concentrated Outcome-Blind search in one early successful lineage. | Lineage rooted at `exp_000001` contained 27 of 28 root-baseline wins. | Direct observation | One trajectory; concentration may depend on early stochastic outcomes. |
| Explicit outcome feedback cannot be assigned a strong causal effect from the proposal-memory runs. | Single outcome-aware and outcome-blind trajectories; different recorded baseline provenance. | Direct observation | A paired, replicated ablation would be required. |
| A nominal 300-second budget produced variable optimizer-step and token exposure. | Calibration steps 20, 21, 20, 23; tokens 10,485,760–12,058,624. | Direct observation | Four repetitions on one hardware/software setup. |
| Calibration BPB varied over a range of 0.018040. | Four values: 1.906045, 1.902874, 1.905279, 1.888005. | Direct observation | Four runs do not fully characterize the distribution. |
| Step/token exposure was a material contributor to baseline variation. | Two 20-step runs differed by 0.000766; the 23-step run was substantially lower at 1.888005. | Suggestive | Observational calibration; other runtime effects may co-vary with step count. |
| Cross-run own-baseline win rates are not strict causal comparisons. | Provenance mismatch plus calibration exposure variation. | Direct observation | Within-run root comparisons remain descriptive facts. |
| Parent-relative analysis is preferable here to counting all descendants below one recorded root threshold. | Lineage inheritance analysis and calibration findings. | Suggestive | Small local deltas still require repeated evaluation to distinguish signal from run noise. |
| Structural diversity and proposal diversity are distinct. | Multi-node credit DAG with 29/30 proposals in one parameter lineage versus later mechanism-family audits. | Direct observation | Mechanism families are curated labels rather than a unique mathematical partition. |
| Better Merge compatibility and synthesis are promising future targets. | Rejection counts and low Merge parent-relative success across runs. | Hypothesis | Not tested by a dedicated learned compatibility or synthesis intervention. |
| Multi-generation credit may allocate budget differently from direct-child credit. | Current credit uses only direct valid children; lineage concentration observed. | Hypothesis | No multi-generation credit experiment was run. |
| Fixed-step or fixed-token evaluation would reduce the observed exposure confound. | Calibration identifies variable completed steps/tokens under wall-clock stopping. | Hypothesis | A redesigned evaluation protocol has not yet been executed. |
